of small-business attacks

Most attacks on a small business aren't genius hacks — they walk through a door that was left unlocked. Here are the seven locks. None cost much, and most take an afternoon.

What's inside

  1. Turn on multi-factor authentication — on your email first.
  2. Switch on automatic updates — everywhere.
  3. Back up your data — and test that it actually restores.
  4. Use a password manager — a different password for every account.
  5. Give each person their own login — with the least access they need.
  6. Confirm money or banking changes by phone — and stop email spoofing.
  7. Encrypt your laptops — and lock down your Wi-Fi.
