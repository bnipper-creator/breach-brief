Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Breach Brief ("we," "us"), an independent educational cybersecurity content brand, handles information in connection with this website and the software applications we use to publish our own videos to our own social media accounts (the "Apps," e.g. "Breach Brief Publisher" on Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Google/YouTube).

The short version

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data about visitors or about other people. Our Apps exist only to publish Breach Brief's own content to Breach Brief's own accounts. They do not read, collect, or process the data of any other platform user.

This website

This site is static informational content. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. We do not collect names, emails, IP-based profiles, or any personal information from visitors. If you choose to email us, we receive only what you send.

Our publishing applications

To post our videos automatically, our Apps use the official APIs of YouTube/Google, Meta (Facebook & Instagram), TikTok, and LinkedIn. For each platform:

  • We authorize the App against accounts we own or control and grant it only the permissions needed to upload and publish our own posts (for example, content-publishing scopes).
  • The resulting access/refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are not transmitted to any third party other than the respective platform's API.
  • The Apps do not access, request, collect, store, analyze, or sell any other user's personal data, messages, followers, or analytics beyond what the operator needs to manage Breach Brief's own posts.

Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we use your message solely to respond. We do not add you to any marketing list and do not share your message.

Third-party platforms

When you interact with Breach Brief on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn, your activity is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not this one.

Data retention & deletion

Because we do not collect personal data from visitors, there is generally no personal data for us to retain or delete. If you believe we hold any information about you, or you want any data associated with our Apps removed, email blakegnipper@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion and we will address it promptly. You may also revoke our Apps' access at any time in your platform's account settings.

Children

Breach Brief is intended for business owners and adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; we will revise the effective date above when we do.
